AuditionMusic5 - Audition Music Streaming & Download

AuditionMusic5 is a fan-made music library for Audition players. Browse tracks from eight Audition servers. Login is required to play, and you can download the tracks you want to keep.

What is AuditionMusic5?

AuditionMusic5 began as a simple way to find music from Audition Online without opening the game. The site collects publicly available track lists from supported servers and presents them in a searchable music library. You can preview a track, let it play in the browser, or download the original file.

The goal is straightforward: make it easier to rediscover old favorites, check a track name before a match, and keep a personal copy of the music you enjoy. Login is required to play tracks, and the site works on both mobile and desktop.

What you can do

  • Switch between the eight supported Audition servers.
  • Search by singer, title, music code, or BPM, and sort by singer, music name, or BPM.
  • Filter the library by All Music, Ballroom Music, or Guitar Music.
  • Play, Shuffle, Repeat & Loop tracks in the browser.
  • Use Guitar Music mode, where backing and note audio are played together.
  • Save All Music tracks to a personal cross-server playlist. Login is required to add or remove tracks.
  • Download TBM, MP3, and OGG tracks. Login is required for MP3 and OGG downloads.
  • Look for the NEW badge to spot the latest music updates.
  • Use the site on mobile or desktop, including offline access.
  • Talk to the admin through live chat.

How the site works

When you choose a track, AuditionMusic5 prepares a playable version for the browser and caches recently used audio locally on your device. Downloads are generated from the same track data. No game installation is required, and recently used tracks can be accessed again in offline mode.

Accounts and privacy

Login is required to play music. MP3 and OGG downloads also require login. Account details are handled according to our Privacy Policy. You can request deletion of your data through the Data Deletion page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AuditionMusic5?

A free web player that lets you listen to songs from the dance game Audition Online without installing the game. It runs entirely in the browser.

Is it really free?

Yes, the site is free to use. Playing music requires login (AM5 account, Facebook, or Google); downloading the original TBM file needs no login.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in the browser, and the song is fetched from the origin server and processed on our server.

What are ABM and TBM files?

ABM and TBM are the proprietary music container formats used by Audition's patch servers. AuditionMusic5 loads them into OGG Vorbis, which browsers can play natively.

Which servers are supported?

Eight: Indonesia (AyoDance), Vietnam (VTC Game), Thailand, China (9You), Korea (Hanbiton), Taiwan (MangoT5), Philippines (NextGen), and Axeso5 (Latin America).

Can I download songs?

Yes, tracks can be downloaded as TBM, MP3, or OGG. TBM downloads need no login; MP3 and OGG downloads require login.

Can I save a playlist?

Yes, you can save All Music tracks to a personal cross-server playlist, but login is required to add or remove tracks.

Who owns the songs?

All music belongs to the respective game publishers. AuditionMusic5 is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by any publisher.
