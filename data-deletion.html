Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to ask AuditionMusic5 to delete the data we store about you. It covers every way you can sign in: an AM5 account, Facebook login, and Google login.

What We Store

  • AM5 account: your username, password, and role (VIP / User).
  • Facebook login: the name, username, and profile link that Facebook shares when you sign in. We never receive your Facebook password.
  • Google login: your name and email address from the Google account you used. We never receive your Google password.
  • Activity records: date and time of logins and visits, IP address, country, and browser type (standard server logs).
  • In your browser only: a login session cookie and your site preferences (selected server, volume, last played song).

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to dev@auditionmusic5.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • how you signed in: AM5 username, Facebook name, or Google email address; and
  • whether you want your account record deleted completely or only your activity records.

Nothing else is needed - no ID, no password. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove AuditionMusic5 from your Facebook account at Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, which stops any further sign-in.

What We Delete and When

  • Within 7 days we delete your entry from our user lists (users, Facebook users, Google users).
  • Within the same 7 days we delete or anonymise your entries (username, IP address) in the login and visit logs.
  • We reply to your email as soon as the deletion is done.
  • Your browser cookie is removed when you log out or clear your browser data - we cannot delete it from your device for you.

What Cannot Be Deleted

  • Your Facebook or Google account itself - that belongs to Facebook/Google, not to us.
  • Anonymous statistics that no longer identify you (for example, total visitor counts) and data we are required by law to keep.

Contact

Questions about this page or about your data: dev@auditionmusic5.com.
