VIP Benefits

  • Access to hidden server options, including private servers and servers that are no longer active.

FAQ

How long does activation take?

Usually 6 to 12 hours. It can be faster depending on whether the admin is online.

What should I send after paying?

Your username, the plan you paid for, and proof of payment. Send all three in live chat.

What if I send the wrong amount?

Pay at least the exact plan price. The network fee is paid by the sender, and we must receive the full amount - a shortfall means the payment cannot be confirmed.

Does my song request quota reset?

Yes. Quotas apply per subscription period and reset at the start of each new period. Unused requests do not carry over.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Message us on live chat and the admin will handle the change manually.

Is VIP required to use AuditionMusic5?

No. Playing, searching, and downloading are available to logged-in users. VIP adds the extra features listed above.
